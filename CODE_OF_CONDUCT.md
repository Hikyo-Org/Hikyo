# Hikyo code of conduct

Hikyo welcomes people of every background and experience level. Everyone taking
part in the project deserves respectful treatment, including people with
disabilities and people whose identity, beliefs, or circumstances differ from
your own.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and make room for questions and mistakes.
- Respect people's names, pronouns, privacy, time, and communication needs.
- Accept moderation decisions and stop an interaction when asked.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, unwanted repeated
contact, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information
without consent are unacceptable. Retaliating against someone for reporting a
concern or helping with a report is also unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

This policy applies to Hikyo's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents Hikyo in public.
The [maintainer set](./GOVERNANCE.md#roles-and-powers) is responsible for applying
this policy. The current sole maintainer is Marc Went (`@Dunky13`).

## Reporting a concern

For content in this repository, open the content's three-dot menu, choose
**Report content**, and choose **Report to repository admins** when offered.
Repository content reports are enabled and can be reviewed by repository admins.
See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

If that route is unavailable, or the concern involves the sole maintainer,
[report abuse to GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
GitHub handles these reports under its own policies; this is an escalation
outside the project's moderation process.

Do not open a public issue to report an incident or post private evidence in a
public conversation. Include links to relevant content and a description of
what happened in the private report. Share only the personal information needed
to assess it. Security vulnerabilities have a separate
[private reporting process](./SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).

## Enforcement

Maintainers review available evidence and context before acting. They may ask
for clarification, remove harmful content, close or lock a conversation, or
restrict participation. Depending on the severity and repetition, consequences
may include a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious
threats or harassment may warrant an immediate ban.

Maintainers must handle reports discreetly and share identifying details only
when needed to investigate or act. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised,
including where GitHub or legal obligations require disclosure. Where possible,
moderation decisions should explain the conduct at issue without exposing the
reporter or other private information. A person contesting an action should
provide relevant context through the same private reporting route; public
retaliation is not an appeal.
