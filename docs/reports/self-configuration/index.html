D22What prevents garbage collection of the running config?A first-class runtime retention reference with a narrow lifecycle.+
Decided under your delegated approval

Options considered

A
Bounded, explicit runtime retention rootsRecommended · selectedProtect the desired, node-active and recovery revisions.
B
Ordinary expiring workload pinRisk the running payload expiring while the process remains healthy.
C
Disable all retentionKeep every secret revision forever.

Why this recommendation

An applied revision can stop being the latest published snapshot, and normal pins have finite lifetimes.

Decision

Amend retention explicitly: protect the desired snapshot, every snapshot still used by an admitted node or unfinished job, and the previous completed activation for recovery. Acquire/release these references under the same snapshot/GC locks as publication. Serialize applies; refuse another new target while an unresolved partial activation would grow the retained set. Keep at most three distinct runtime-referenced payloads in steady single-flight operation. Stale nodes are fenced before their references are released; they cannot resume an old generation. Surface retained-by-runtime in history, then return released payloads to normal retention. Never recycle a normal pin into a hidden infinite pin. Retention roots and GC are confined to the owning instance; no root-view reference retains or copies another instance’s payload.

Required proof

GC racing apply/rollback cannot collect required payloads; hundreds of completed applies do not retain hundreds of system revisions.

Step 1 of 5

Before durable commit

Resolve an exact revision, validate against the current runtime catalogue and prepare all admitted nodes. Any failure here keeps the existing target and runtime active. No SMTP probe is sent.

After durable commit

The new generation is the durable target. Nodes swap and acknowledge it. A missing acknowledgement means pending or partial, not success. Restart resumes this target; stale nodes must reconcile or refuse affected work.
