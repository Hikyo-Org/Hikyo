Conservation and load reduction

Five maintenance entry points become reliability, retaining daily security, Monday 04:00 race, Monday 05:00 GitLab/reporting, and Monday 06:17 channel checks. Two native manual acceptance entries become release-acceptance; PR-capable operations remain separate. Routing rejects unknown events and suite selections.

The required core job exercises every benchmark once in crypto, dotenv, scanning, server, and service after compiling/testing those packages. This removes its separate smoke runner without deleting benchmark execution. The separate three-iteration scanning guard remains. Smoke adds serial work to the full core job; compilation reuse is not a measured hosted wall-clock improvement.

The frozen full-domain/execution baseline remains unchanged. Exact additive #884 demo and #886 benchmark steps require independent assertions and refusal mutations. Tests for removed legacy producers transfer their applicable source, permissions, cache, fork, trigger, and failure guarantees to active workflows. No test, race, fuzz, security, signature, native hardware, release, or paid-budget gate may be dropped to obtain green.

The completion-driven gate uses short callbacks and queued Checks API results. ci-control, ci-fork, and ci-gate-reconcile retire after hosted proof and an atomic required-context swap. Same-name legacy/new writers must not coexist as alternative success authority. Branch rules match check names and optional app identity, not producer workflows; the generic GitHub Actions app cannot establish stronger workflow-origin enforcement. Manual inspection of the exact trusted publisher/full-candidate provenance remains mandatory. This platform limit predates the split and is not claimed solved here. The bounded binary v2 identifiers and conservative truncated-v1 invalidation repair remain compatible with deployed checks.
