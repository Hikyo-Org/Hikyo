## Design Context

Users and brand personality are defined canonically in `PRODUCT.md` (its Users
and Brand Personality sections). This file adds only the docs-site design
specifics below.

### Aesthetic Direction

Industrial and documentation-first. Use Hikyo's graphite/linen dual theme,
restrained teal accent, Instrument Sans for interface and body typography, and
IBM Plex Mono for code, keys, and values. Prefer hairlines, compact geometry,
and strong type hierarchy over cards, shadows, gradients, glow, or decorative
motion. Dark is the default; light is fully supported.

### Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious.
- State support and security boundaries in plain language.
- Keep policy source canonical and visibly linked to implementation.
- Use color only with text or glyphs; meet WCAG contrast and 44px touch targets.
- Keep pages fast, static, self-contained, and usable on narrow screens.
